# User research

Interview 5–10 other hackers early (PRD section 2). Record real answers only. **Never invent statistics.**

## Questions

1. How do you track deadlines today?
2. Have you ever missed or mis-submitted something? What happened?
3. Where do deadline changes reach you (email, LMS announcement, class, Discord)?
4. What would you trust an agent to do for you?
5. Would you let it submit finished work for you? What would make you comfortable?
